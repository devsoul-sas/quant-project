Protocol identity: `S4.4-ACCU-PRIMARY-V1`, SHA-256 `658d0a063e3087712ba580bc546fa014aa24d9ddaf8fea17c0a760961d49f670`.

The primary scope was frozen before acquisition: R_75 normal, growth rates 1%, 3%, and 5%, primary horizon 3 ticks, three explicit normalized rules, and an unconditional baseline. It also fixes feature windows, chronological partitions, purge, seed, moving-block bootstrap, familywise correction, abstention semantics, stop conditions, and zero-contract policy. No fitted value or provider-derived parameter is stored in the protocol.

`accu_research_engine.py`:

- acquires one tick sequence per instrument and shares it across growth-rate evaluators;
- computes causal features and labels future survival separately;
- resets state at discontinuities;
- uses chronological train/validation/holdout partitions with purge;
- calibrates probabilities on train only;
- evaluates coverage, absolute lift, Brier score, idealized economic expectation, moving-block uncertainty, and multiplicity in memory;
- clears the mutable tick buffer in `finally` and never serializes metrics or provider data.

Reused components: `Tick`, `SessionDeadline`, `fetch_ticks_history`, `propose_accumulator`, Decimal-based domain conventions, and the validation/fail-closed patterns of `campaign_spec.py` and `research_runner.py`. `multiplier_lab.py`, `adaptive_intelligence.py`, and `robustness_statistics.py` were inspected but not coupled because their contracts do not represent ACCU barriers, censored FIFO state, or this Decimal block interval.

## 7. Power and S4.5 proposal

The pilot cap is not a confirmatory sample. For a future single frozen hypothesis, a conservative planning example with baseline 0.85, two-sided familywise alpha 0.05 over nine tests, 80% power, 2 percentage-point minimum detectable lift, and design effect 2 requires about 8,329 effective rule entries. At 10% rule coverage and a 20% holdout, that is about 416,444 ticks, or 231.4 hours at two-second cadence. A 5-point target still requires about 1,333 effective entries (roughly 66,650 ticks or 37 hours under the same assumptions).

S4.5 should therefore choose one primary rule and one growth rate before acquisition, justify an empirical dependence/design effect using owned synthetic or authorized ephemeral diagnostics, then size the holdout from the declared minimum effect and anticipated coverage. It must preserve the same read-only, zero-contract, deadline, cleanup, and multiplicity gates. No S4.5 work was started.
