## Subject

Commercial licence clarification for persistent quantitative research and derived models

## Message

Hello Twelve Data team,

DevSoul is evaluating Twelve Data as the licensed historical-data source for an internal,
provider-independent quantitative research system. Development is currently local and uses
owned synthetic fixtures. We will not ingest or persist Twelve Data content until the licence
scope is confirmed in writing.

The intended use may include:

- automated historical downloads for selected markets;
- persistent internal storage and reproducible backtesting;
- conversion to research formats and retention of backups;
- causal features, labels, aggregates, metrics and backtest reports;
- training and retention of models, parameters and checkpoints;
- private presentation of aggregated results to a development partner;
- a possible future customer-facing SaaS, which would be separately licensed;
- no resale of a raw market-data feed unless expressly licensed.

Could you provide a written proposal or order form that answers the following for the specific
datasets and exchanges selected?

1. Which Business/Enterprise tier permits permanent retention of historical data? What exact
   TTL applies to raw historical data, caches, logs, queues, replicas, snapshots and backups?
2. May we transform the data into Parquet/CSV and build persistent research datasets?
3. What contractual test determines whether features, labels, aggregates and metrics are
   non-reversible `Derived Data`?
4. Are trained models, weights, parameters, checkpoints, signals and predictions treated as
   Derived Data? May they survive deletion of the source data and termination?
5. May backtests and aggregated evidence reports be shown privately to a development partner?
6. Which tier/add-on permits customer-facing charts, alerts, signals and reports?
7. Which rights are required for API responses, exports or downloads exposed to customers?
8. Which exchanges/third-party providers require separate agreements, declarations or fees?
9. On termination, what must be deleted, within what deadline, and what audit fields may be
   retained? Please address raw data, Derived Data, models, logs and backups separately.
10. What attribution, exchange notices and branding requirements apply?
11. Can the agreement cover a Colombian developer/business, and which contracting entity,
    governing law and dispute terms would apply?
12. Please identify the datasets, endpoints, rate limits, service levels, monthly/annual price
    and all exchange/add-on fees included in the proposal.

We are interested first in internal historical research. Any future external display or SaaS
would remain disabled until separately covered in writing.
