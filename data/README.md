# Local data

Do not persist Deriv market data here. Under the API Users Terms reviewed on 2026-09-29, API/site content may only be cached for up to 24 hours unless separate written permission applies.

Any temporary Deriv cache must use an operational TTL of at most 23 hours, have no backups, and fail closed if deletion cannot be verified.

Only DevSoul-owned synthetic data or data from a source with a documented compatible licence may be retained. Never store credentials, PATs, OAuth tokens, OTP URLs or account payloads here.
